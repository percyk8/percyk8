 I identify as a Classical Archaeologist, but primarily focus on the [Late Bronze Age through the Early Iron Age on Crete](https://archaeopresspublishing.com/ojs/index.php/JGA/article/view/2354). Most of my fieldwork revolves around legacy data from the [Sir Arthur Evans Archive](https://www.ashmolean.org/collections/article/sir-arthur-evans-archive) dealing with the site of [Knossos](https://knossospalace.gr/). 
 
I am a fourth-year PhD candidate in the [Mediterranean Art and Archaeology Program](https://archaeology.virginia.edu/people/kaarin-percy) at the University of Virginia.I also have been given the opportunity to participate in the [Praxis Program](https://praxis.scholarslab.org/) Fellowship at UVA for the 2026-2027 academic year.

I dedicate most of my free time to my position as co-president of the UVA organization [“You’re Not Alone.”](https://sites.google.com/view/youre-not-alone-uva-at-uva?usp=sharing&utm_source=ig&utm_medium=social&utm_content=link_in_bio&fbclid=PAcGRvZgJleHRuA2FlbQIxMQBzcnRjBmFwcF9pZA85MzY2MTk3NDMzOTI0NTkAAadksm42n-TLDhqnbdnDGUZ1yC6ljvtI4XM7Ej1LoDdcCa1LQq4wfSkaSgXvYQ_aem_AE4oTIVw4Fg4cmtbPCWErA&pli=1&authuser=0) YNA’s mission is to build community among survivors of sexual assault at UVA and in the Charlottesville area. The group is open to any and all advocates and survivors.
